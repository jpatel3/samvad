Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my reading progress?

Go to Settings > Reset Progress. This will reset all progress for the current scripture track. Note that this action cannot be undone.

Can I switch between Vachanamrut and Bhagavad Gita?

Yes. On the home screen, tap the scripture selector at the top to switch between tracks. Your progress is saved separately for each scripture.

How do I change the language?

Go to Settings > Language and choose between English, Gujarati, or Hindi. The change applies immediately to all readings.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. All scripture content is included in the app. No internet connection is required to read, listen, or track progress.

How does the text-to-speech feature work?

Tap the play button at the bottom of any reading to hear it read aloud. You can also tap on individual dialogue blocks to hear just that passage. The app uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine.

How do I switch between light and dark mode?

Go to Settings > Appearance and select Light or Dark.

Is my data stored online?

No. All your data (progress, streaks, settings) is stored locally on your device. Nothing is sent to any server. See our Privacy Policy for details.
