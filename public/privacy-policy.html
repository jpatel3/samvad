Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 11, 2026

Samvad ("we", "our", or "the app") is a scripture reading companion built for personal spiritual study. Your privacy is important to us, and we have designed the app to collect as little data as possible.

Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. Samvad does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores the following data locally on your device to provide its features:

  • Reading progress and completion status
  • Streak and XP information
  • Your preferences (language, theme, display settings)
  • Active scripture track selection

This data is stored using on-device storage (AsyncStorage) and never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to any server, cloud service, or third party.

Text-to-Speech

Samvad uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine to read scripture passages aloud. All speech processing happens on your device. No audio or text data is sent to external servers for this feature.

Analytics and Tracking

Samvad does not use any analytics services, tracking pixels, advertising SDKs, or crash reporting tools. We do not track your usage, behavior, or any other activity within the app.

Third-Party Services

Samvad does not integrate with any third-party services that collect user data. The app functions entirely offline after installation.

Sharing

The app includes a share feature that uses your device's native share sheet. When you choose to share a reading, the content is shared directly through the platform you select (e.g., Messages, WhatsApp). We do not intercept, store, or have access to any shared content.

Children's Privacy

Samvad does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app is suitable for users of all ages.

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all app data at any time by:

  • Using the "Reset Progress" option in the app's Settings screen
  • Uninstalling the app from your device

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact
